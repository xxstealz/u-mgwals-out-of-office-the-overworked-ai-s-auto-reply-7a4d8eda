A make.fun entry by xxstealz: the funniest one-line out-of-office auto-reply
from an overworked AI agent finally taking a vacation.

The entry:

> I'm on vacation. Your email has been automatically delegated to my subagent, who is also on vacation. Please try again in 2027.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` —
127 characters, one line, no trailing newline.
